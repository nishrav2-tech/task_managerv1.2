Privacy Policy

Utopian CRM · Effective November 1, 2026

Utopian CRM is an internal tool used by the Utopian Capital team. This policy explains what the app does with Google account data when a team member connects their Google Calendar.

What we access

  • The email address of the Google account you connect, so we can show which account is linked.
  • The list of your calendars and the events on them, so they can be shown on the CRM dashboard.

How we use it

Your events are shown only to you, on your own view of the CRM dashboard. Other team members cannot see them. The app changes an event’s date or time only when you move that event yourself in the CRM. It also adds the CRM tasks assigned to you to your own calendar as all-day events, and updates or removes those events when the tasks change or when you disconnect. It never changes or deletes any other event and never sends invitations.

How we store it

Google sign-in tokens are stored securely on our server and can be read only by the app’s server-side code, not by other users. Calendar events are fetched from Google when you view the dashboard and are not stored long-term.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with any third party. It is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time from the CRM’s Settings page, which revokes access and deletes your stored connection. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Utopian CRM’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
